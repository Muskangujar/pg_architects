Our Story
Our Story

Founded on the belief that design transforms lives.

PGD Architects is a full-service architecture, interior design, and planning firm. We craft holistic design solutions that seamlessly blend indoor and outdoor environments, creating functional and aesthetically compelling spaces.

Our firm brings a multidisciplinary approach to every project, from conceptual planning to detailed execution, with a commitment to sustainability, innovation, and human-centric design principles.

The Founder

Phalguni Gujar-Doshi

Founder & Principal Architect · B.Arch

With over 5 years of collective experience across architecture, interior design, landscape, and project management, our team at PGD Architects is driven by a passion for creating spaces that are both visually inspiring and functionally exceptional.

Our multidisciplinary expertise enables a holistic design approach, from the macro scale of urban planning and township layouts to the micro details of residential interiors and bespoke furniture.

Our Approach

You can make meaningful change
through design. Let us show you how.

We follow a structured, collaborative process that transforms your vision into reality, from the first sketch to the final walkthrough.

01

Discover

Understanding your vision, context, and functional requirements through in-depth consultation.

02

Design

Translating ideas into spatial concepts, 3D visualizations, and detailed design documentation.

03

Develop

Refining every detail: materials, finishes, engineering coordination, to ensure build-readiness.

04

Deliver

On-site supervision and quality assurance to bring the designed vision to life, exactly as imagined.
